### 2021 -- The Summer of Exploits

The most turbulent year in THORChain history. Three exploits in 18 days, plus a full network halt from a token parsing bug.

**May 13 -- Halt Mechanism Limitations.** `Halt%sChain` only halted inbounds, not outbounds. Critical gap: funds could bleed out on a halted chain.

**May 17 -- Centralization vs Safety Debate.** _sonofodin: "Mimir should NEVER be between a user and their funds. Halting a chain should require a supermajority, never a single person."

**May 21 -- Stuck BTC / 3.8 BTC at Risk.** Yggdrasil return stuck on BTC due to high fees. Churn delayed ~5 days.

**May 25-28 -- ETH Stuck / Slash Points Crisis.** Yggdrasil vault stuck on ETH. Bifrost auto-cancel failed. Nodes accumulating slash points.

**Jun 2 -- Midgard SPOF.** Just 2 AWS instances in Germany with naive DNS load balancing.

**Jun 3 -- ETH Sync Stuck Post-Update.** Multiple nodes stuck 95-140 blocks behind on ETH.

**Jun 7 -- Repeated ETH Daemon Failures.** Persistent slashing from stuck sync.

**Jun 20 -- 9 Nodes Out of Sync on BTC / Near-Halt.** "Failing to do so will result in loss of funds. If not actioned asap, the network may need to be halted."

**Jun 24 -- MKR Symbol Bug / Full Network Halt (High).** MKR returns hex bytes as token symbol. Bifrost panicked, halted all ins/outs. Mimir halted ALL trading across all chains. Fix in v0.56.1.

**Jun 28 -- HACK #1: ETH Parsing Exploit (~$140K stolen, Critical).** First major exploit. Attacker used custom attack contract on ETH router. Bifrost did not distinguish router events from called transactions. Network halted within ~1 hour. Funds declared safe overall.

**Jul 7 -- HACK #2: ERC777/XRUNE (~$15M returned, Critical).** Whitehat extracted all XRUNE from ETH contract. Returned funds minus bounty. Network halted to prevent further loss.

**Jul 15 -- HACK #3: Live ETH Attack (~$5M stolen, Critical).** ~4,000 ETH extracted. Attacker could have stolen BTC/BEP2 but chose not to. Community halted THORChain within ~1 hour. Treasury covered stolen amount. Three bugs: (1) smart contract, (2) slow response, (3) ETH chain should have been halted earlier.

**Jul 19 -- ETH Chain Paused Pending Audit.** ETH chain officially paused until v0.62.0.

**Jun 30-Jul 1 -- Synth Exploit Disclosed.** Synth limits bypassable by adding liquidity, minting, then removing.

**Jul 14 -- Stuck Pool Creation.** RUNE leg succeeded while asset leg failed. Affected MKR, LINK, BAT pools.
