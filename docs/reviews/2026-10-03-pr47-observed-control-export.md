The projection includes collection/checked/assessment dates, provider URLs, requested/observed response-height evidence, presentation quality, warnings, reviewed control states and raw Mimir integers. Missing runtime identity stays null; capturing does not read api/version or add any provider request. Export time does not renew stale or retained evidence.

The scope is public control evidence already loaded by the page. Node/address records and quotes are omitted. Raw numeric strings preserve precision; unsupported values remain unavailable, zero remains zero. Oversized keys are omitted rather than leaking through a nominal value bound. Unknown or unsupported pinning remains unverified, independently of the sample height. Output is ordinary inert data, not provider attestation, transaction instructions or archival recording.
