Reviewed 2026-10-03 against THORNode v3.20.3 commit `b08d81f79275093b0fcb753e0d68ff1c16c51cb8`. [Immutable manager source](https://gitlab.com/thorchain/thornode/-/blob/b08d81f79275093b0fcb753e0d68ff1c16c51cb8/x/thorchain/manager_dynamic_fee_current.go), full-file SHA256 `ed62525b7b52a5d9ede561183bc91338a238bf37a527ddb6ec3de81668eae474`, independently retrieved during the PR40/41 review.

`CreditSwap` (lines106-142) selects one `dominantEligibleAffiliate` in active or monitor state and credits that affiliate/pair accumulator. `creditDynamicFeeForLegs` (lines147-185) iterates eligible L1/RUNE swap legs, uses inbound or emitted RUNE plus liquidity fee as appropriate, and converts volume and liquidity fee using DollarsPerRune. Missing conversion prevents credit. This does not support a claim that every partner receives the same swap attribution. A multi-leg transaction and a complete protocol revenue ledger are also different observation units; the loaded bounded attribution histories cannot attest complete protocol revenue.

The existing loader is bounded. [History source](https://gitlab.com/thorchain/thornode/-/blob/b08d81f79275093b0fcb753e0d68ff1c16c51cb8/x/thorchain/types/type_dynamic_fee.go), SHA256 `182f6b4f8dbfc9a6ab6391007746c298df2f9cb52c935898136e0d051085115f`, retains at most30 records per attribution. Missing epoch IDs can result from inactivity, retention or incomplete loading. They are not backfilled as zeros.

The page groups by epoch and affiliate/pair key. Identical unexpected duplicate copies contribute once; conflicting copies contribute no numeric value and disclose missing coverage. These are defensive handling of response copies, not a claim that the protocol deliberately duplicates partner credit. Available stored amounts are summed exactly in base units and partial fields remain visibly partial. Real zeros remain zero. The common-cohort switch selects keys present in every loaded epoch and does not restore missing fields or omitted history.

`bps_at_close` is a stored controller floor. Its displayed arithmetic mean is explicitly unweighted, not an effective fee rate. The unequal-volume fixture (100 and1 base units, bps2 and10) has mean6; no volume-weighted or compound return is inferred. Chart lines connect only consecutive epochs with identical membership and complete controller-floor coverage. Gaps and membership changes remain separate points. No causal revenue-lift conclusion is drawn.

Validation: five unit regression scenarios cover partial fields, membership changes, common-cohort filtering, empty intersections and valid zeros, identical duplicates and conflicting copies. The browser journey checks visible field coverage before and after switching to common membership on desktop and mobile. Final build and browser results are recorded in the implementation ledger and PR.
